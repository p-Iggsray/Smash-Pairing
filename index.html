Local-first PWA. No tracking.

How to play
  • Add player names to Experienced (orange) or Inexperienced (blue) columns.
  • Pick a mode from the menu — Full 2v2 balances skill across teams, Split 2v2 keeps skill levels apart.
  • Tap Generate to create pairings.
  • Tap a team name to rename it; tap a player to swap with someone on the bench.
  • Use Pair Waiting to match up players sitting out the round.
  • Reset Teams clears just the current pairings — your roster and presets stay.
